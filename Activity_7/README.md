## About this Activity
In this activity, we were tasked to use two AI tools in generating a resume that contained our actual personal information. With both, we were tasked to compare and contrast, and finally utilize the ideas and design principles of the LLMs in creating a resume.

<div align="center">
  
## What I've Learned
- I learned that LLMs are useful in making a resume, given that they could organize and arrange provided information accordingly.
- I personally didn't like the fact that I needed to recall my roots in order to provide information for the proper generation of my resume. I overcame it by actually putting in the time and effort to trace back the actual dates and years of specific events.
