## About this Activity
In this activity, we were tasked to create a brand and incorporate a personal branding on it, including its logo, tagline, color palette, and typography. This is based on Lesson 2 where we learned about the principles of having your own personal brand and how to stay consistent with it.
<div align="center">
  
## What I've Learned
- I learned more about the things to consider when you want to build your own personal brand. Emphasizing the need for unity and harmony within your design, and remaining consistent to incorporate your own mark in the minds of your viewers.
- I've had trouble in choosing the colors and typography for my brand because I couldn't focus and come up with a certain theme that would have complementary colors, but I overcame this with by researching more about color palletes and choosing the colors that I thought reflected the created business properly.
- I could use this in future endeavors where branding should be taken seriously, like in career-making, marketing, business, and many more.
