# $\color{purple}{\textsf{Activity 3: Social Media Infographics and Project Documentation}}$

<div align="center">

## About this Activity
In this activity, we were tasked to create an infographic about a societal issue, and provide a project documentation on my propes solution for it. I chose Corruption to be the subject of my infographic and the project documentation is centered about combatting the issue with a digital platform that encourages the truth and transparency in government projects and officials.
<div align="center">
  
## What I've Learned
- I learned to turn how to turn a complex societal issue like corruption into clear, evidence-based visuals and a practical solution. It allowed me to apply design frameworks and branding principles from the previous activities
- I had trouble in compressing the details of the concept of corruption into a digestible inforgraphic, but I overcame this by actually nitpicking the information I want to emphasize because a complex societal issue like corruption really has a lot of important factors
- I could use this in future works such as projects or other crafts where awareness, transparency, and documentation should be emphasized.
