# $\color{purple}{\textsf{Activity 1: Presentation Design Principles}}$

## About this Activity
In this activity, we were tasked with making a flyer containing an ad for a free coffee event at Ateneo de Davao University. This ad should showcase the application of presentation design principles, and it is grounded in the first lesson about the framework of digital design and media.

<div align="center">
  
## What I've Learned
- I learned the characteristics of a presentable presentation in digital media and the important design principles, and I gained more knowledge about the editing tools for digital media, and its proper usage, this includes tools like Canva, google workspace, and many more.
- I experienced difficulty in coming up with a design that properly incorporated the presentation principles but I overcame it by letting my creative side go wild, rather than thinking too systematically. At the end of the day I still made a design that fitted properly to the rubric and criteria of the activity.
- I could apply this in future endeavors where I have to enhance or tamper with digital media for marketing, designing, and many more!
