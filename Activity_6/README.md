# $\color{purple}{\textsf{Activity 6: Making a Website}}$

<div align="center">

## About this Activity
In this activity, we were tasked to create a website, using the aid of LLMs. The website I created is an advocacy website aimed to increase awareness in the rampancy of corruption and injustice in the Philippines, and it also serves as a call for action towards the issue.

<div align="center">
  
## What I've Learned
- I learned that LLMs are more than just tools to answer your queries and that it could also help us create bigger projects such as websites.
- I experienced difficulty in conceptualizing my website, but I overcame it by basing it on relevant issues in today's society.
- I could apply this in future endeavors that require me to market and promote a specific something.
